How to play

Tuck runs to the right on his own. Tap anywhere to hop; keep your finger down to hop higher. Land on a frostmite to stomp it: it packs into a snow block where it stood, and you can stand on that block to reach ledges and walls too high to jump. Spiky critters cannot be stomped. Lanterns on the trail save your spot. Each level has three snowflakes: reach the cabin, find all three mittens, and finish without a fall. From level 5, a Snow Globe rewinds three seconds after a fall, and the scarf button puts on a Cozy Scarf that shields you from one bump.

Purchases

World 1 is free. All Worlds ($3.99, one time) opens worlds 2 and 3. The Globe Pouch ($0.99) and Globe Chest ($2.99) add 5 or 15 Snow Globes and Cozy Scarves; you can also trade glints for them in the Shop. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases (also in the Shop). Booster packs are used up when spent and cannot be restored.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
