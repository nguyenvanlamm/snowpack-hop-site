Snowpack Hop

Privacy Policy

Snowpack Hop · last updated 23 October 2026

Snowpack Hop is a platform game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the snowflakes earned in each level, your best glint count per level, and your Snow Globes, Cozy Scarves and glints.
  • Your settings: whether vibration is on.
  • Whether you have bought All Worlds, and which store purchases were already delivered (so a purchase is never counted twice).

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

World 1 is free. All Worlds and the booster packs are bought through the App Store, which handles payment under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
