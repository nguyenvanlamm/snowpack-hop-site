Snowpack Hop

Stomp the snow critters. Build your way up.

Every critter is a step

Stomp a frostmite and it packs into a solid block of snow right where it stood. Some walls are too high to jump, so the critters are your stairs.

Pick your step

Choose which critter to stomp and where. A block in the wrong place is a step to nowhere.

New tricks each world

Waddlers, hoppers that leave blocks in the air, gliders, and spiky critters you cannot stomp at all.

Lanterns save your spot

Light a lantern on the trail and a fall sends you back there, not to the start.

How to play

One thumb, a few minutes a level, a lit cabin at the end.

1

Tap to hop

Tuck runs on his own. Touch anywhere to jump.

2

Hold to go higher

Keep your finger down for a longer, higher hop over gaps and ledges.

3

Stomp and climb

Land on a critter, then use the block it leaves to reach the next ledge.

What you get

36 levels, 3 worlds

Pinefall, Frostridge and Aurora Peak, each longer and trickier than the last.

Three snowflakes a level

Reach the cabin, find all three mittens, and finish without a fall.

Two helpers

A Snow Globe rewinds three seconds after a fall. A Cozy Scarf shields you from one bump. Glints you collect trade for both.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every level plays without a connection. The App Store is used only when you buy something or restore a purchase.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads and no ad networks.

What does it cost?

World 1 is free. All Worlds is a one-time $3.99. Booster packs are optional, and boosters can also be earned in play.

What data do you collect?

None. There is no account, and progress is saved on your device only.

Which devices?

iPhone. It is played in portrait with one hand.
